# Santa Cruz Pizza Delivery 2

**Santa Cruz Birthday Airlift II** is a five-delivery arcade flight campaign built for Mark's birthday.

Fly a Joby Pizza aircraft across a real Santa Cruz map, dodge organized seagulls and rogue delivery drones, answer a fully voiced cast, and bring the final pizza-cake to Bonny Doon.

## Controls

- Mobile: press and hold anywhere around the aircraft to steer; release to coast.
- Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to steer.
- Boost: Space or the Pizza Afterburn button.
- Land: hold E or hold the landing button inside the yellow target zone while below 72 mph.
